{{/* FACET contact_gate version: 2.0 | status: stable | layer: 2 The gate for a message written to a person you already know the name of, and it renders INSIDE the conversation, directly under the message that did not land. That placement is the whole feature. Somebody who has never heard of a F33D3R Number will never find one in Settings; they will find it here, at the only moment they have a reason to care — they wrote something, it did not get through, and the explanation and the way forward are under their own words. inputs: Handle string — the account the viewer wrote to Decision string — "request" | "deny" | "unavailable" | "malformed" PendingID string — the id of the message the SERVER is still holding for this pair. Present, the Number affordance is offered and submitting a Number sends THAT message. Absent, the gate explains and offers nothing, because there is nothing to send. states: request | deny | unavailable | malformed children: none signals: receives: POST /messages/send (a message that could not be delivered) POST /messages/new-number (a Number that did not open the door) emits: POST /messages/new-number → replaces the whole thread pane with whatever the server decides: the conversation, or this gate again. context: messages → thread pane, inside gnosis_prospect_thread notes: WHAT MAY BE NAMED AND WHAT MAY NOT. The handle may be named: the viewer typed it, and it is on a public profile. The recipient's POLICY may never be named. Whether the wall was followers, mutuals, a Number expected and not presented, a contact link not held, or a door closed to everybody is the recipient's business, and a sender who could read it off this fragment would be reading how somebody has locked their door. "deny" IS ONE STATE AND MUST STAY ONE STATE. It is what a policy refusal, an unknown Number, a revoked Number, an expired Number, an exhausted Number and a Number belonging to somebody other than this person all render as — identical words, identical markup. The authority already collapses them to a single `deny` with a single reason; collapsing them again here is what stops this surface being the enumeration oracle the rest of the lane refuses to be. The handler holds every one of them to the same floor on the clock, so they are also indistinguishable by timing. Do not add a helpful sentence naming which it was. "malformed" MAY be named, and only it. Whether what somebody typed is shaped like a Number at all is arithmetic they can do offline without this service, so saying so discloses nothing — and it is the difference between a person fixing a typo and a person concluding they were turned away. "request" IS NOT A REFUSAL and must never read as one. On F33D3R a contact IS a mutual follow; there is no contacts list and no address book. Three tiers reach somebody: a mutual follow goes through freely, a Number passes that Number's gate, and anyone else knocks. This state is the knock, and the message is still here waiting on the answer. The Number field carries no format. Length, alphabet and grouping belong to canonicalNumber and to the brain that mints Numbers, so nothing here can drift out of step with them: no pattern, no maxlength, no example. It is inputmode="numeric" rather than type="number" so a phone offers the keypad while a Number that is not purely digits can still be typed. The contact link is here because capability_only is otherwise an unreachable policy: somebody holding a link and no other route needs a field to put it in. The note is here, and it is NOT the message. The message stays undelivered until the recipient agrees to receive it; a knock that carried its own payload would have delivered itself and "accept" would be a formality after the fact. The note is the short, optional thing a sender writes to say why they are asking, and it is the only thing about them that crosses before the recipient decides. */}} {{define "contact_gate"}}
