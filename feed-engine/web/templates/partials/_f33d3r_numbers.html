Your F33D3R Numbers

A Number is how someone reaches you without your phone number, your email or your address book. Say it aloud, put it on a badge, print it on a flyer. Anyone who has it may ask to reach you under the rules you set for that Number — and learns nothing else about you. Retire one at any time: your account, your keys and every open conversation are untouched.

The Number stops letting new people in when the event is over, whether or not you remember. Anyone already talking to you is unaffected.
Say it to a room of forty and it stops at forty. People who already follow you both ways do not count against it — they never needed the Number. Every new Number carries a limit unless you choose otherwise: a Number with no limit is one anybody who finds it can keep using for ever, and that should be a decision rather than an accident.
