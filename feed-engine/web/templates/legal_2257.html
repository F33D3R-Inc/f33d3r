18 U.S.C. § 2257 Record-Keeping Requirements

Last updated: 2025

Custodian of Records

F33D3R Platform maintains records as required by 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75 for all adult content creators. All performers depicted in adult content on this platform are 18 years of age or older at the time of production.

Record Location

How Records Are Stored

F33D3R Platform stores records as cryptographic decision hashes — we never retain images of identification documents. Age verification is performed via our Verity compliance brain, which hashes documents client-side before any data leaves the user's device. Only a SHA-256 fingerprint of the document is transmitted.

Creator Obligations

Creators who post adult content on F33D3R certify that all depicted persons are 18 years of age or older. By enabling adult creator mode, creators grant F33D3R Platform permission to maintain compliance records on their behalf as required by 18 U.S.C. § 2257.

This page constitutes the required compliance statement pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75. F33D3R Platform does not produce, distribute, or host any content depicting minors. All sexually explicit content on this platform is user-generated by adult creators who have completed age verification.
